# ACT I — Grief and Recognition · 00:00–08:00

## SC01 · A Promise at the Grave
`00:00–02:00` · `SH001–SH010` · **GRAVE RIDGE** · Night/dusk

Ember's immense body curls beside a human-sized grave. The ruined village remains distant,
not forgotten. A paw settles beside the headstone; a single tear slips down his cheek.

**One** clearly warm memory insert shows human Elara **alive** in the established forest
location — never as a present-day ghost. Her remembered gentleness contrasts with what
Ember did in anger. He returns to the cold grave. A path and faint footsteps prepare SC02.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Key line** | EMBER (V.O.): *"Mom... I'm still here."* |
| **Optional memory line** | ELARA: *"Be gentle, my son."* — new scripted memory dialogue, **not** a quote from Part 1 |
| **Plants** | The vigil · the guilt · the approaching path |

## SC02 · The Princess Who Stayed
`02:00–04:00` · `SH011–SH020` · **PINE TRAIL → GRAVE RIDGE** · Day

Aurelia walks the forest route with two background royal escorts. At the ridge the escorts
hold at a respectful distance while she sees the grave and the tears. She is afraid, but she
reads **mourning**, not an active threat. She does not touch him without permission. Ember
turns away. She leaves space rather than insisting he trust her.

> Her kindness begins as **recognition**, not immediate romance.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Key line** | AURELIA: *"I won't come closer. I only wanted you to know you're not alone."* |

## SC03 · A King Chooses Restraint
`04:00–06:00` · `SH021–SH030` · **CASTLE — HALL** · Day

Aurelia asks her father not to hunt Ember. The King names the cost of the burned village and
his duty to protect people. She does not deny it: *"Then let him choose something better."*
He grants guarded visits under clear conditions.

He briefly shows an old illustrated book — **no readable AI-generated page text** — and
mentions the rare tale: love and freely chosen protection changing a dragon's shape. He
treats it as a tale, not a rescue plan or a guaranteed spell.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Key lines** | KING: *"Some old tales say love can change a dragon's shape. It cannot be forced."* <br> AURELIA: *"I am not trying to change him."* |
| **Plants** | ⭐ **The transformation rule** — so SC10 is a payoff, not a deus ex machina |

# ACT II — Compassion Becomes a Risk · 08:00–18:00

## SC05 · A Price on Compassion
`08:00–09:36` · `SH041–SH048` · **QUARRY CAMP / PINE TRAIL** · Day

The scout observes the princess's recurring forest route. At the quarry camp the leader sees
ransom as a route to power and money. They assume Ember will never leave the grave. The
enforcer prepares rope and moves the fallen log into trap position beside the known trail.

> Establish the **three distinct silhouettes** and the **fixed camp layout** here.

## SC06 · The Ambush
`09:36–12:00` · `SH049–SH060` · **PINE TRAIL → QUARRY CAMP** · Late afternoon

Aurelia returns along the same route with her escorts. The trap blocks the path; attackers
separate the guards from her. **Guards are stunned or pinned, not casually killed.** Aurelia
resists and leaves a visible trace beside the known split boulder. They take her to the camp
in the late afternoon. She tests a knot and watches the gate — **actively seeking escape.**
The ransom demand is prepared.

> Camera coverage breaks the action into single readable beats, **not one overloaded generation.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Key line** | AURELIA: *"My father will not reward you for this."* |
| **Plants** | The split-boulder trace → confirms the route in SC07 |

## SC07 · The Ransom and the March
`12:00–14:00` · `SH061–SH070` · **CASTLE / PINE TRAIL** · Dusk

The King receives the demand before nightfall. His fear is personal, but he **organises

## SC08 · Her Cry, His Mother's Farewell ⭐
`14:00–18:00` · `SH071–SH090` · **QUARRY CAMP / RAVINE / GRAVE RIDGE** · Night

**The turn of the film.** Four minutes carrying five beats — budget them:

1. Aurelia loosens a binding and reaches for escape, and is discovered.
2. The enforcer re-secures the loose wrist loop — **visibly explaining her later restraints.**
3. She cries ***"Help me, Ember!"*** The sound echoes through the established adjacent ravine.
4. Ember hears the recognisable voice and rises in distress — torn between the danger there
   and the promise he made here.
5. **Elara's spirit appears once**, beside her grave, with the same approved face and clothes.
   The farewell is kept **concise**.

> EMBER: *"Mom, I love you!"*
> ELARA: *"I cannot return, my son. But you do not lose me by loving someone else."*
> EMBER: *"I promised I would stay."*
> ELARA: *"That princess truly loves you. Go save her. It is my final wish."*

Elara adds a brief reminder of mercy. She touches his lowered head. **He kisses the
headstone — not a newly living body — and leaves.** Her spirit fades. Her death is not undone.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Payoffs** | SC04's hearing · SC01's vigil |
| **Plants** | Elara's mercy reminder → SC09's *"No more."* |
| **Hard rule** | No resurrection. The spirit **releases**, it does not return. |

# ACT III — Strength Without Cruelty · 18:00–24:00

## SC09 · Strength Without Cruelty ⭐
`18:00–22:00` · `SH091–SH110` · **QUARRY CAMP** · Night

**The climax of character, not of spectacle.**

Ember reaches the fixed camp. The bandits threaten Aurelia. Anger offers the old answer:
*burn everything.* Instead he creates a safe space, shields her, disables the weapon rack,
and destroys the camp's **fighting capacity**.

> **Production:** cut from controlled fire to the **prebuilt scorched state** rather than
> asking one AI clip to simulate an entire collapsing camp.

When an enemy drops a weapon, **Ember stops.** The Captain and vanguard arrive at the
rear-right gate and witness him protecting human lives — including people he could have
killed. The leader is secured for royal justice. Aurelia is freed outright, not left
dependent on a bargain.

> AURELIA: *"Ember! I'm here!"*
> CAPTAIN: *"Hold your weapons. He's protecting her."*
> EMBER: ***"No more."***

| | |
|---|---|
| **Payoff** | Part 1's *"Don't hurt anyone"* — Elara's rule, finally chosen rather than obeyed |
| **Function** | Earns the Captain's testimony in SC11 |

## SC10 · Love Before the Miracle
`22:00–24:00` · `SH111–SH120` · **QUARRY CAMP** · Night

Aurelia reaches Ember **while he is still a dragon.** She embraces his lowered face:
*"I love you, Ember."* Her tear lands on his scales **only after** the freely chosen rescue
and the mercy. Light rises.

> **Production — critical:** use a **white-gold light transition and a match cut** from an
> approved dragon frame to an approved, fully clothed human frame. **Do not** depend on a
> generic model inventing the correct human face during a long morph.

The princess sees **the same eyes** in a breathtaking new face. Exhaustion, relief and
magical shock overwhelm her; she faints briefly. Ember supports her. The Captain secures the
perimeter and helps the pair reach the nearby river bank.

> **Wardrobe:** approved ivory tunic and dusty-rose cloak. No nude transformation, no new
> random prince design.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Key line** | EMBER: *"Aurelia? Stay with me."* |
| **Order of operations** | choice → mercy → love → tear → change. **Never reorder this.** |

# ACT IV — A Love Carried Forward · 24:00–28:00

## SC11 · A Father Learns to See
`24:00–26:24` · `SH121–SH132` · **RIVER BANK** · Dawn

Human Ember tends Aurelia at the serene river camp. She wakes and recognises **his eyes and
voice**, not just his beauty. The King arrives with the Captain and knights. The Captain
corroborates the rescue and the restraint.

Ember acknowledges what he did in anger **without claiming that one good act erases it.**
The King's change of mind is **earned**. He asks his daughter what she wants, and she makes
a free choice.

> EMBER: *"I cannot undo what I did. I can choose what I do next."*
> KING: *"I judged your shape. I should have watched your deeds."*
> KING: *"Is this your choice, Aurelia?"*
> AURELIA: *"Yes. I loved him before any of this."*

|---|---|
| **Payoff** | SC03's conditions · SC07's march · SC09's witness |

`26:24–28:00` · `SH133–SH140` · **CASTLE COURTYARD → GRAVE RIDGE** · Day

A **restrained** ceremony in the same castle courtyard, with red-rose garlands as a planned
decoration state. Approved faces and costumes throughout.

Later, Ember returns to the same grave, laying fresh red roses. Aurelia stands beside him
**without taking Elara's place.** A **second** visit implies a continuing ritual, not a
single convenient farewell. Elara's approved spirit face appears softly in the clouds as a
poetic final image — **not proof of resurrection.**
