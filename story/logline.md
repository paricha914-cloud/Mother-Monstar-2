# Logline

> **THE MOTHER'S MONSTER: PART 2**

A dragon trapped beside his mother's grave must leave the only place he still feels loved
to save a princess who accepted him as he was. Choosing mercy over revenge opens a future
he never imagined, without undoing the loss of his mother.

---

## Core theme

> **Love does not erase grief. It gives us the courage to carry it into a life that still matters.**

## Elevator pitch

Ember buried his mother and never left her grave. A princess finds him there — and instead
of a hunt, she brings food, silence and company, asking nothing of him. When bandits take
her for ransom, her cry carries across the ravine to the one place he swore never to leave.
Elara's spirit comes once, not to return, but to release him. He storms the camp and breaks
their weapons without breaking their men — and when the last enemy drops his blade, he stops.
Aurelia holds his dragon face and says she loves him. Her tear falls, and the shape that
grief built finally changes. He marries her. He still brings roses to the grave.
