A young woman in a frozen mountain village gives birth to a dragon. The village calls it a monster. She calls it her son — and dies protecting him.

## Story beats — Part 1

### Act One — The Birth
1. Night. Elara runs through the cold, alone, in labour. Screaming.
2. She gives birth — to a **dragon hatchling**. *"This can't be my baby."*
3. Horror turns to acceptance. *"Come here."* She takes him in her arms.
4. She hides him and goes down to the village. *"I'll come back."*

### Act One (b) — The Lie
5. The village asks about the baby. Elara: *"He didn't survive."*
6. *"Where is he?"* — *"I buried him in the mountains."*
7. *"You should have come to us."* — *"I just wanted to be alone."*
8. Marta's suspicion starts here. Nessa defends her: *"Come on, Marta. Leave her be."*

### Act Two — Raising Ember
9. Elara tries to feed him ordinary food. He refuses everything. *"Then what do you eat, little one?"*
10. She realises: **meat**.
11. Marta to Nessa: *"She's lying to us. We need to keep an eye on her."*
12. Elara starts selling her possessions and handmade crafts for meat. *"As much meat as these will buy."*
13. The village notices the quantity. *"That's a lot of meat for just one person."* Her cover story: *"Since I lost the baby, I've been weak. I need to get my strength back."*
14. **The bonding sequence** — Ember bathes in a tub, plays, eats ravenously. Warmth and laughter. The emotional core of the film.
15. Ember **grows at an impossible rate**. *"Ember, how did you get so big so fast?"* — *"I'm going to need a bigger tub soon."*
16. Elara sacrifices her own comfort: *"I suppose I can be cold, but you can't go hungry."*
17. She sells a piece she made herself — *"It took me a long time."* — to Nessa.
18. **Ember learns to breathe fire.** *"Look at you. You learned to make fire."*

### Act Three — Discovery
19. Marta sees Ember above Elara's house. *"I knew it."*
20. Marta to Father Elden: *"Something bad is coming. There's something among us. Something that eats meat. A monster. And I think Elara is hiding it."*
21. **Night search.** Elara hides Ember — *"Crouch against the wall."* They find nothing.
22. *"You searched my home. There's no monster here. Now get out."* — *"This isn't over. I know what I saw."*
23. Father Elden: *"Go home and rest, Marta. At first light, we'll see what you saw."*

### Act Four — The Mob
24. Ember eats **Marta's sheep**. At dawn: *"Elara's monster. It ate my sheep!"*
25. Marta rouses the whole village. *"She's been hiding it from all of us."*
26. The mob at Elara's door: *"Come out, witch."* / *"You gave birth to a monster."*
27. Ember emerges. Elara: *"No, Ember, don't hurt anyone, please."*
28. *"She controls that creature. She is a witch."*
29. Elara proves he's gentle: *"Look, he's not hurting me."* The mob: *"You're with the witch, too."*
30. Violence. Ember defends them and injures attackers. Elara, desperate: *"Ember, no. Please don't hurt anyone."*
31. *"Ram, tie it with the rope."* They rope Ember. *"No. Please stop. Don't touch him."*
32. Elara chooses his life over hers: *"Ember, you have to fly. You have to go."* / ***"Take care, my little baby."***
33. Ember escapes into the sky.
34. *"I'll deal with him later. First, I'll finish you."* Elara: ***"He'll come back for me."***
35. **They kill Elara.** *"God will be pleased with me. I killed a witch."*

### Act Five — The Burial
36. *"We cannot bury her in our graveyard. She was a witch."* — *"Then throw her into the valley behind the village."*
37. **Ember returns.** The village scatters in terror. *"She raised this monster. This is her fault."* / *"Kill the monster. Drive it away."*
38. Ember finds Elara's body in the valley.
39. **The dragon buries his mother.** *(The director has named this the emotional peak of the film.)*
40. Memory montage in Elara's voice: *"Don't hurt anyone, Ember. Just go."*
41. Final line / promise: ***"I will come back."***

## World rules established

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Setting** | A frozen mountain village. Pre-industrial, isolated, walled with gates. |
| **Authority** | Religious — a priest (Father Elden) holds moral power; accusations are framed as witchcraft. |
| **Ember's diet** | Meat only. Refuses all other food. |
| **Ember's growth** | Extremely fast — hatchling to large flying dragon within the film. |
| **Ember's abilities** | Flight, fire-breath (learned on screen), great strength. |
| **Ember's nature** | **Not evil.** Obeys Elara, restrains himself, acts only in defence of her. |
| **Economy** | Barter — goods and handmade crafts traded for meat. |
| **Unexplained** | *How* a human woman gave birth to a dragon. Never answered in Part 1. |

## Themes

- A mother's love against an entire world
- Who the real monster is — the creature, or the crowd
- Fear dressed as faith; the mob as a moral animal
- Sacrifice: Elara gives up food, warmth, possessions, reputation, and finally her life
- The innocent made guilty by what it looks like

---

## Tone & visual language

- **Palette:** cold — snow, grey stone, blue night; broken by fire and firelight (Ember's warmth vs. the mob's torches)
- **Scale:** intimate domestic scenes (the tub, the hearth, feeding) escalating to a mass mob climax
- **Register:** dark fantasy, emotionally direct, grief-forward — not action-forward
- **Dialogue:** plain, simple, spoken English. Short lines. Repetition used for emotion (*"Go, Ember. Go."*)
- **Audio:** heavy use of breath, panting, snorts, screams; score carries the montages
