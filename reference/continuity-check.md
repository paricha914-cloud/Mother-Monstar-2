# 🟢 Consistent

## G1 · "Don't hurt anyone" → "No more." ⭐

**The strongest thing in the draft.**

| Part 1 | Part 2 |
|---|---|
| Elara, under attack: *"Ember, no. Don't hurt them."* He **obeys**. | Ember, mother dead, revenge available, enemy disarmed: ***"No more."*** He **chooses**. |

Part 1 established restraint as *obedience*. Part 2 converts it into *character*. This is exactly how a sequel should pay off a setup — and SC08's "brief reminder of mercy" from Elara's spirit is the hinge that makes it explicit.

**No action needed. Protect this at all costs.**

## G2 · Fire is learned, not innate
Part 1 makes fire-breath an on-screen milestone (*"You learned to make fire"*). Draft v03 has Ember using **controlled, aimed** fire in SC09 — consistent with a learned, practised skill rather than an innate blast. ✅

## G3 · Extreme growth rate
Part 1: hatchling → large flier within one film; *"How did you get so big so fast?"*
Draft v03 SC01: *"Ember's immense body curls beside a human-sized grave."* ✅ The size contrast is both consistent **and** the best image in the film.

## G4 · Meat diet
Part 1: refuses all non-meat food; meat is the plot engine.
Draft v03 SC04: Aurelia offers food, he refuses, later accepts a little. ✅

> 💡 **Free win:** specify it is **meat**. Aurelia working out what he eats quietly rhymes with Elara's discovery in Part 1 — same gesture, different woman, no dialogue needed.

# 🔴 Decide before scripting

## R1 · Does dragon-Ember speak out loud?

**This is the biggest one.**

| Part 1 | Draft v03 |
|---|---|
| Ember is **entirely non-verbal.** Snorts, cries, growls. He understands Elara perfectly and never speaks a word. | SC01 *"Mom... I'm still here."* is marked **(V.O.)** ✅ <br> SC08 *"Mom, I love you!"* / *"I promised I would stay."* — unmarked <br> SC09 ***"No more."*** — unmarked <br> SC10+ *"Aurelia? Stay with me."* — **human form, fine** ✅ |

Your SC01 marking suggests the intent is interior voice. But SC08 and SC09 are unmarked, and *"No more."* in particular **reads** like it is said aloud to the bandits.

### Three clean options

| Option | How it works | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **A — All interior** ⭐ | Every dragon-form line is `(V.O.)`. The audience hears him; characters don't. The Captain's *"He's protecting her"* reads his **actions**, which is stronger anyway. | None. Fully Part-1 safe. |
| **B — Spirit exception** | He speaks only with Elara's spirit in SC08 (spirit-to-spirit). SC09's *"No more."* stays `(V.O.)`. | Slight rule-fuzz, but emotionally lovely. |
| **C — "No more." is said aloud** | One single spoken word-pair, the only one in two films. Devastating if earned — but it **does** contradict Part 1's non-verbal dragon unless the time gap explains it. | Needs a beat somewhere establishing he can now speak. |

> **Recommendation: A, with C as a deliberate, discussed exception.** If you take C, give it a reaction shot — the bandits' faces registering that *the dragon just spoke* is worth more than the line itself.

**→ Action: mark every dragon-form line in the Fountain script explicitly as `(V.O.)` or not. No ambiguity.**

## R2 · The burned village — **VERIFY AGAINST YOUR FOOTAGE**

Draft v03 treats *"the burned village"* as established fact, and it is **load-bearing**:
Ember's guilt (SC01), the King's objection (SC03), principle 1, and his SC11 line all rest on it.

**But I could not verify it.** Part 1's transcript ends with: villagers scatter, *"Kill the monster. Drive it away."* — then Ember finds Elara's body and buries her. **A transcript cannot show fire.** So:

| If… | Then |
|---|---|
| ✅ Ember visibly burns the village in Part 1 | **Nothing to do.** Perfect continuity. |
| ⚠️ It isn't on screen | SC01's *distant ruined village* + one short memory/insert beat covers it cheaply |
| ⚠️ It happened between films | Say so once, in the King's SC03 lines |

> 💡 **If he did burn it — that's a gift, not a problem.** It means Ember has *already taken his
> revenge* and knows exactly what it tastes like. *"No more."* then isn't naivety, it's a man
> who has been there refusing to go back. Lean into it.

**→ Action: confirm from your own cut, then tell me which, and I'll write SC01/SC03 accordingly.**

# 🟡 Needs a decision or one line

## A1 · "Valley" vs. "Grave Ridge"

| Part 1 | Draft v03 |
|---|---|
| *"Then throw her into **the valley** behind the village."* Ember buries her there. | The location is **GRAVE RIDGE**, with the ruined village *distant*. |

Not a contradiction — a ridge can sit above/within a valley, and your Geography section puts everything in *"one connected winter valley."*

**→ Action: lock one slugline and use it everywhere.** Suggested: `EXT. THE GRAVE RIDGE — VALLEY OF THE OLD VILLAGE`, or simply keep **GRAVE RIDGE** and have the King refer to *"the valley"* once in SC03 to tie them.

## A2 · Who made the headstone?

Part 1: a **dragon** buried her, with claws, in a dumping ground. No marker shown.
Draft v03: SC01 has *"the headstone"*; SC08 *"He kisses the headstone."*

**→ Recommendation:** make it a **rough standing stone Ember dragged upright himself** — unworked, uncarved, obviously placed by something enormous. Costs nothing, needs no dialogue, and makes the vigil deeper in a single shot. (Alternatively, leave it; most viewers won't ask.)

## A3 · Marta, Father Elden, Ram and Nessa are unaccounted for

Part 1's three named killers **survive** — as does **Nessa**, the one villager who was kind. Draft v03's fixed cast includes none of them and the village is ruined.

Part 1's audience *will* ask. Options:

| Option | Cost |
|---|---|
| **Deliberate silence** — the ruined village in the background says it | Free |
| **One line from the King in SC03** naming what was lost ⭐ | One line; also sharpens his objection |
| A Nessa cameo | ❌ Blocked by *"no new speaking characters"* |

**→ Recommendation: the King's line.** It closes the loop and strengthens SC03 at the same time.

## A4 · Time gap is undefined

Nothing states how long after Part 1 this is. It affects Ember's size, the ruin state of the village, and how much Aurelia already knows.

**→ Action: pick a number and state it once.** *"One winter"* fits the snow, the ruin state, and Ember's growth rate.

## A5 · Was Part 1's village inside this kingdom?

Part 1's village is walled, isolated, and **priest-led** — no king, no knights, no royal justice. Draft v03 has a kingdom, a castle, a Captain and royal law.

| If… | Dramatic consequence |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Yes, same realm** | The King's *own subjects* burned a woman as a witch and he never came. His *"duty to protect people"* becomes self-indicting, and his SC11 *"I judged your shape"* gets much heavier. |
| No, outside his authority | Cleanly explains why no royal justice ever came for Elara. Simpler. |

**→ Recommendation: Yes.** It's free depth and it makes the King's arc cost him something.

## A6 · Acute hearing is new lore

Not established in Part 1 — but Draft v03 **plants it properly** in SC04 (the bird) before paying it off in SC08. That's correct construction.

**→ Action: none. Just make sure the SC04 plant is unmissable** — it carries the entire causality of the midpoint.
