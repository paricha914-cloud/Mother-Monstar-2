## 1. GRAVE RIDGE ⭐

| | |
|---|---|
| **Slugline** | `EXT. GRAVE RIDGE - DAY / NIGHT` |
| **Scenes** | SC01, SC02, SC04, SC08, SC12 — **5 scenes, the most used location** |
| **Mood** | Cold, still, sacred. The only place Ember feels loved. |

**Description:** A snow-covered ridge holding a single **human-sized grave** and a headstone.
Ember's immense body curls around it. The **ruined village** is visible in the distance —
*distant, not forgotten.* A path and faint footsteps lead up from the pine trail.

| Day / clear | SC02, SC04 |

**Continuity:** ⚠️ Part 1 calls this *"the valley behind the village."* Lock one name — see

## Causality — why the geography matters

| Fact | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Camp is **across an adjacent ravine** from the grave | Aurelia's real cry can reach Ember (SC08) |
| Ember's hearing is planted in SC04 | The cry is physics, not telepathy |
| Ransom reaches the King **late afternoon** | Enough daylight to organise before dark |
| Captain's vanguard goes **on foot**, faster | He arrives during SC09 — no coincidence |
| King takes the **harder snowy route** | He arrives at the river at **dawn** (SC11) |
| River bank is **near the camp** | Exhausted pair can plausibly reach it |
| **No horses, no mounted characters** | Nothing new to design |
