## 1. ELARA'S COTTAGE

| | |
|---|---|
| **Slugline** | `INT./EXT. ELARA'S COTTAGE - DAY/NIGHT` |
| **Part 1 use** | Where Ember is hidden and raised; the tub; the hearth; the night search; the final siege |
| **Mood** | The only warm place in the film |

**Description:** A small house at the edge of the village. Stone and timber. A hearth, a wooden tub, Elara's craft tools. Progressively emptied as she sells everything to buy meat.

## 5. THE VALLEY BEHIND THE VILLAGE ⭐

| | |
|---|---|
| **Slugline** | `EXT. THE VALLEY - DAY` |
| **Part 1 use** | Elara's body is thrown here, denied the graveyard. **Ember buries her here.** |
| **Mood** | Sacred ground made by a dragon |

**Description:** The dumping ground for the unburiable — turned, by Ember's own hands, into the only grave that matters in the film.

**Part 2 note:** The single most emotionally loaded location in the franchise. Any return to the village should route through here.
