# ELARA

> *"Take care, my little baby."*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Protagonist of Part 1 — the Mother |
| **Status** | **Dead** (killed by the mob at the end of Part 1) |
| **Species** | Human |
| **Home** | A cottage on the edge of the frozen mountain village |
| **Part 2 presence** | Memory, flashback, voice-over, grave. Possibly more. |

---

## One line

A young woman alone in a frozen village who gives birth to a dragon, hides him, starves for him, and dies refusing to give him up.

## Appearance

Young. Worn down over the course of the film — she sells her clothes and possessions and goes cold and hungry to feed Ember. Start warm and whole, end thin and grey.

## Personality

Quiet, private, stubborn. Not a warrior — her strength is refusal. She never once considers abandoning Ember, from the moment she first says *"Come here."*

## Want vs. Need

- **Wants:** to keep her son alive and hidden.
- **Needs:** to be *seen* — to have someone other than Ember recognise that she is not a monster. She never gets it. (Nessa comes closest.)

## Arc

| Stage | State |
|---|---|
| Beginning | Alone, pregnant, terrified |
| Discovery | Horror → instant, total acceptance: *"Come here."* |
| Middle | Secret motherhood. Joy. Sacrifice escalating to starvation. |
| Climax | Chooses Ember's life over her own: *"You have to fly. You have to go."* |
| End | Murdered as a witch. Denied burial. Buried by her son. |

## Key relationships

| Character | Relationship | Tension |
|---|---|---|
| Ember | Son | She must teach a predator not to kill — and he obeys her |
| Marta | Accuser | Marta's suspicion is the engine of the plot |
| Nessa | The nearest thing to a friend | Nessa buys her goods, defends her once, but does not save her |
| Father Elden | Authority | Converts suspicion into sanctioned violence |

## Voice

Plain, short sentences. Pleads rather than argues. Repeats herself under pressure (*"Go, Ember. Go. Go."*). Lies badly and only to protect him.

## Signature lines

- *"This can't be my baby."* → *"Come here."*
- *"I suppose I can be cold, but you can't go hungry."*
- *"Look, he's not hurting me."*
- *"Take care, my little baby."*
- *"He'll come back for me."* ← **her last line, and a promise Part 2 owes the audience**

## Part 2 notes

Her grave in the valley behind the village is a location Ember dug himself. Her voice is the natural source of Ember's conscience in Part 2 — the restraint he was taught (*"Don't hurt anyone"*) against the revenge he's owed.
