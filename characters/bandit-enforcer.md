## One line

The one who sets the rope, moves the log, and re-ties the knot.

## Function

He is the film's **continuity instrument.** Every physical restraint in Act II is traceable to him, which is what stops the audience asking *"wait, wasn't she untied?"*

| Scene | Action | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| SC05 | Prepares rope; moves the **fallen log** into trap position beside the known trail | The trap in SC06 is pre-built on screen, not conjured |
| SC06 | Executes the ambush; the guards are **stunned or pinned, not killed** | Keeps the bandits criminal, not cartoonishly murderous |
| SC08 | Catches Aurelia mid-escape and **re-secures the loose wrist loop** | ⭐ **Visibly explains her later restraints** — the draft calls this out explicitly |
| SC09 | Physical threat during the rescue; disarmed, spared on surrender | Tests principle 5 |

## Personality

Blunt, physical, obedient to the leader. Not a sadist — a labourer of violence. This matters: when he drops his weapon, Ember stopping must feel like mercy toward *a person*.

## Design note

One of **three distinct silhouettes** — the heaviest/broadest of the three. Must be readable in night-camp lighting.
