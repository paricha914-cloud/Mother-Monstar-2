## One line

A ruler who is right about the danger and wrong about the creature, and who is honest enough to say so.

## The thing that makes him work

He is **not a tyrant and not an obstacle-by-stupidity.** His objection is legitimate: Ember burned a village. The film never pretends otherwise (principle 1). He grants guarded visits — a real concession under real conditions — and he changes his mind only when given **evidence** (the Captain's testimony) and his **daughter's free choice** (principle 7).

## Personality

Measured, burdened, protective. A man who thinks in terms of duty to people rather than pride. His fear for his daughter is personal and visible — and does not make him stupid.

## Arc

| Stage | State |
|---|---|
| SC03 | Judges by shape. Grants a conditional mercy he does not really believe in. |
| SC07 | Terrified father who still issues a disciplined, merciful order (*"Take prisoners if you can"*). |
| SC11 | Judges by deeds. Hands the decision to his daughter. |

## Voice

Formal but plain. Short declarative sentences. Never shouts. Admits error without ceremony.

## Signature lines

- *"Some old tales say love can change a dragon's shape. It cannot be forced."* (SC03)
- *"Bring my daughter home. Take prisoners if you can."* (SC07)
- *"I judged your shape. I should have watched your deeds."* (SC11)
- *"Is this your choice, Aurelia?"* (SC11)
