# EMBER

> *"I will come back."*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | The dragon. Elara's son. **Protagonist of Part 2.** |
| **Status** | Alive. Alone. Flew from the village after burying his mother. |
| **Species** | Dragon (born of a human mother — mechanism unexplained in Part 1) |
| **Father** | **UNKNOWN — the reveal of Part 2** |

---

## One line

A dragon raised by a human mother who taught him not to kill — now orphaned by the people who taught him why he should.

## Nature

**Ember is not a villain.** Part 1 is explicit about this:

- He obeys Elara, even mid-attack.
- He only ever acts in her defence.
- He eats Marta's sheep out of hunger, not malice — and that single act kills his mother.
- He hides when told to hide. He flies when told to fly.
- He returns for her body and buries her.

Any Part 2 that makes him a simple monster betrays Part 1.

## Abilities (established on screen)

| Ability | Notes |
|---|---|
| Flight | Established mid-film |
| Fire-breath | **Learned on screen** — *"You learned to make fire."* A milestone, not innate |
| Extreme growth rate | Hatchling → large flying dragon within Part 1. *"How did you get so big so fast?"* |
| Great strength | Overpowers armed villagers |
| Carnivore | Meat only. Refuses all other food. Appetite scales with growth. |
| Understanding | Understands Elara's speech and obeys it. Non-verbal — snorts, cries, growls. |

## Arc in Part 1

| Stage | State |
|---|---|
| Birth | Helpless, hungry, rejected by the world |
| Childhood | Loved. Playful. Bathed in a tub. Taught restraint. |
| Adolescence | Powerful, hungry, impossible to hide |
| Loss | Roped, driven off, returns too late |
| End | Buries his mother. Alone. Promises to come back. |

## Arc available in Part 2

The whole film is the tension between two instructions:

| Elara's teaching | The world's lesson |
|---|---|
| *"Don't hurt anyone."* | They killed her anyway. |

Plus the teaser's two new pressures: **someone has written down what he really is**, and **he has a father**.

## Design notes

- Scale must have grown again since Part 1 — a time-skip is fully justified in-world by his established growth rate.
- Keep the warmth: Part 1's visual signature is Ember as *fire and warmth* against a cold blue village. He is the only heat in the film.
- Non-verbal performance. All emotion through body, breath and eyes.

## Signature moments

1. Learning to make fire — pure delight
2. Being roped while Elara screams at him to flee
3. Burying Elara in the valley
4. *"I will come back."*
