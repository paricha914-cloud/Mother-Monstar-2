# NESSA

> *"Come on, Marta. Leave her be."*

| | |
|---|---|

---

## One line

The villager who buys Elara's handmade things, defends her once, and does nothing when it matters.

## Function in the story

Nessa is the film's moral middle. She:

- Trades with Elara and treats her decently
- Defends her against Marta early on
- Buys the handmade piece Elara spent a long time making — *"It's beautiful."*
- Still notices the meat: *"That's a lot of meat for just one person."*
- Does not stop the mob

She is complicity-by-silence, which is a different crime from Marta's.

## Personality

Warm, practical, non-confrontational. Not cruel — just not brave.

## Signature lines

- *"Come on, Marta. Leave her be."*
- *"It's beautiful."*
- *"Of course. I'm sorry."*
- *"That's a lot of meat for just one person."*

## Part 2 notes

**The highest-value survivor.** The natural bridge character:

- She owns something Elara made with her own hands — a physical object carrying Elara's scent/memory, and the only tangible thing left of her besides the grave.
- Guilt gives her a real arc: the one who could have spoken and didn't.
- If Part 2 needs a human the audience can stand beside while Ember returns, it is Nessa.
