# MARTA

> *"I know what I saw."*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Antagonist of Part 1 — the Accuser |
| **Status** | **Alive** at the end of Part 1 |
| **Species** | Human |

---

## One line

An elderly villager whose suspicion — correct in every detail — destroys an innocent woman.

## Function in the story

Marta is the engine. Every escalation in Part 1 starts with her:

1. Doubts Elara's story about the dead baby
2. *"She's lying to us. We need to keep an eye on her."*
3. Sees Ember above the house
4. Brings it to Father Elden — converting a suspicion into a religious matter
5. Her sheep is eaten — the spark
6. Rouses the village at dawn

## The uncomfortable truth

**Marta is right.** There *is* a monster, Elara *is* hiding it, and it *did* eat her sheep. Part 1's power comes from the fact that the villain is correct about the facts and monstrous about the conclusion.

## Personality

Watchful, certain, unshakeable. Frames her fear as warning and her cruelty as duty.

## Signature lines

- *"She's lying to us."*
- *"Something is wrong... Something that eats meat. A monster. And I think Elara is hiding it."*
- *"This isn't over. I know what I saw."*
- *"It ate my sheep."*

## Part 2 notes

One of three named survivors who are complicit in Elara's murder. Prime target of Ember's return — and therefore the prime test of whether Ember keeps his mother's rule.
