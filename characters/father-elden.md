# FATHER ELDEN

> *"At first light, we'll see what you saw."*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | The village priest — authority that licenses the killing |
| **Status** | **Alive** at the end of Part 1 |
| **Species** | Human |

---

## One line

The priest who turns one woman's suspicion into the village's permission to murder.

## Function in the story

Father Elden never throws a stone. He does something worse: he makes it holy.

- Marta brings him her fear; he legitimises it
- He authorises the search of Elara's home
- He sets the dawn deadline — *"At first light, we'll see what you saw"* — which is effectively the scheduling of her death
- The killing is justified in his language: *"God will be pleased with me. I killed a witch."*
- He rules on the body: *"We cannot bury her in our graveyard. She was a witch."*

## Personality

Calm, paternal, reasonable in tone — which is what makes him frightening. He never raises his voice.

## Signature lines

- *"Go home and rest, Marta."*
- *"At first light, we'll see what you saw."*
- *"We cannot bury her in our graveyard."*

## Part 2 notes

The teaser's line — ***"someone has written down what he really is"*** — points straight at an institution with **books**: a church, a scholar, a chronicler, a hunter's bestiary.

Father Elden is the existing character closest to that world. Either he wrote the record, or the person who did outranks him. Strong candidate for the bridge between Part 1's small village and Part 2's bigger antagonist.
