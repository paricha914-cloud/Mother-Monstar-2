## One line

A man who correctly calculates everything about the dragon except the one thing that matters.

## The thesis line

His SC05 line is the engine of the whole second half — and the film's best piece of dramatic irony:

> *"A grieving beast won't leave his dead."*

He is **right about the grief and wrong about the love.** Ember leaves the grave precisely because of what the leader counted on him never doing.

## Motivation

Ransom as a route to **power and money** — not chaos, not cruelty for its own sake. A planner. He sees a repeated route, a predictable kingdom, and an immobilised dragon, and runs the numbers.

## Why he is kept alive

Principle 5: *the rescue destroys weapons and camp structures, not every living person.* The leader surviving to face **royal justice** — rather than dragon justice — is the proof that Ember chose. Killing him would cost the film its climax.

## Personality

Cold, commercial, confident. Speaks in terms of value and odds. Never rants.

## Design note

Must read as **one of three distinct silhouettes** (leader / scout / enforcer), established clearly in SC05.
