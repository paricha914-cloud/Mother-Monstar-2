## One line

The one who notices that the princess walks the same path every time.

## Function

Minimal lines, maximum structure. He is the causal link between Aurelia's kindness and her abduction:

```
SC04  she returns, and returns, and returns
  ↓
SC05  the scout observes the recurring forest route
  ↓
SC06  the ambush is placed on a route they know she will walk
```

**The film's cruellest irony runs through him:** her compassion is what makes her predictable, and being predictable is what gets her taken.

## Personality

Watchful, patient, unobtrusive. The quiet one. Reports rather than decides.

## Design note

One of **three distinct silhouettes** — must be instantly separable from the leader and the enforcer at a distance and in low light. Suggest the leanest/smallest of the three.

## Staging note

Place him in SC04's background or in a clean POV insert so the audience registers the watching **before** SC05 explains it.
