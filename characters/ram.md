## One line

The man told to tie the rope — and who does.

## Function in the story

Minor but pointed. *"Ram, tie it with the rope."* He is the ordinary person who carries out the order, which is the film's whole thesis about mobs in one beat.

## Part 2 notes

Expandable if Part 2 needs a human face for the village's guilt at ground level — someone who was neither the instigator (Marta) nor the authority (Father Elden), just the one who obeyed.
