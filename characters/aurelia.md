## One line

A princess who sees a grieving son where everyone else sees a monster — and who loves the dragon before she ever meets the man.

## The thing that makes her work

**She is not a prize and she is not a cure.** Three design choices protect this:

1. She loves and accepts **the dragon**, before any human form exists (principle 2).
2. **She never knows** her love can transform him. She is not courting a hidden prince (supernatural rule).
3. **She acts in her own captivity** — tests knots, watches the gate, leaves a trace at the split boulder. She is looking for her own way out when rescue arrives (principle 2).

## Personality

Brave in a quiet register. Her courage is *restraint* — she holds back, gives space, and does not demand trust she hasn't earned. She argues with her father on moral grounds without denying his facts.

## Want vs. Need

- **Wants:** that Ember not be hunted; later, that he not be alone.
- **Needs:** nothing from him. This is precisely why it works.

## Voice

Direct, gentle, unpitying. Never coaxing or saccharine. Her lines concede the other person's point before making her own.
